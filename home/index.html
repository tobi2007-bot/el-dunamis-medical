Our Story

Built on Faith & Excellence

Founded in 2000 with a vision to make quality healthcare accessible to all, EL DUNAMIS Medical Centre has grown from humble beginnings into a trusted name in healthcare.

For us, healthcare is more than treating illnesses; it is about caring for people, restoring hope, and making a lasting difference in lives. Through the dedication of our medical professionals, compassionate service, and commitment to excellence, we continue to put our patients first.

Every patient we care for is part of our story. Every life touched reminds us why we began.

✦ Medical Director
Leadership & Vision

Dr. Ayansola Jeremiah

Founder & Medical Director

Dr. Ayansola Jeremiah Funso is a distinguished medical practitioner, administrator, and minister with a passion for healthcare excellence and kingdom service. Dr. Funso earned his MBBS degree from the University of Ilorin College of Medicine and has continued to advance his professional expertise through specialized medical training. He is a Clinical Preceptor with the College of Medicine, University of Lagos, and also holds a Certificate in Ultrasonography from the Lagos State University Teaching Hospital. A highly respected healthcare professional, Dr. Funso is a member of the Nigerian Medical Association and the Association of Nigerian Private Medical Practitioners. He is also the Managing Director of EL DUNAMIS MEDICALS LTD , where he provides leadership in delivering quality healthcare services with professionalism and compassion.

Beyond his medical and professional accomplishments, Dr. Funso is deeply committed to raising healthy families, strengthening communities, and promoting holistic wellbeing through both ministry and medical practice. He is happily married and blessed with children.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome walk-ins for general consultations, but booking ahead through our Schedule a Visit page helps us reduce your wait time and ensures the right doctor is available.

EL DUNAMIS Medical Centre is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Both our outpatient clinics and emergency services are available around the clock across all branches, every day of the year.

Please bring a valid ID, your HMO card (if applicable), and any previous medical or immunization records. Antenatal clinic runs every Thursday; immunization clinic runs every Tuesday.
